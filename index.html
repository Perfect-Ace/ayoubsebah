03 — Method

From idea to impact, in five steps

  1. 1

    Discover

    Stakeholder interviews, requirements and a clear definition of success.

  2. 2

    Design

    Architecture, data model, user flows and a realistic plan with milestones.

  3. 3

    Deliver

    Agile sprints, Scrum rituals, transparent progress and early demos.

  4. 4

    Validate

    Testing, model evaluation, user feedback and quality gates.

  5. 5

    Scale

    Rollout, documentation, training and measuring results.
